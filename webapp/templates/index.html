What is the Bias Score? ▾

The Bias Score (0–10) measures how clustered your research perspectives are — mathematically, not subjectively.

1
Each page you visit is converted into a 3D stance vector representing its sentiment, objectivity, and ideological lean.
2
The system calculates the centroid (average position) of all your vectors across the session.
3
It measures the average Euclidean distance from each vector to the centroid.
4
A tight cluster (small distance) = high bias. A spread out distribution = balanced research.
0–3 Balanced — diverse perspectives
4–6 Moderate — some clustering
7–10 Echo Chamber — very similar sources
